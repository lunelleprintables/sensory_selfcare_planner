The Lunelle Neurodivergent Philosophy

Why standard productivity triggers sensory burnout

Three physiological friction points that traditional organizers completely overlook:

⚡

1. Sensory Load Hijacks Executive Function

When sensory stimuli (glare, noise, tactile discomfort) exceed your processing threshold, your brain enters a low-grade fight-or-flight state. Initiating tasks becomes biologically impossible until sensory safety is restored.

🫀

2. The Interoception Blindspot

Autistic and ADHD individuals frequently experience delayed internal awareness: forgetting to drink water, ignoring full bladders, or crashing from low blood sugar. Planners without somatic prompts accelerate physical breakdown.

⏳

3. The Toxic Habit Streak Spiral

Consecutive daily checkboxes create intense anxiety. Missing one day triggers guilt and causes 84% of neurodivergent users to abandon their planners entirely. Lunelle replaces streaks with energy-based pacing.

The Occupational Framework

Human beings have 8 sensory systems, not 5.

Our workbook audits your individual neurological blueprint across all 8 modalities, identifying what drains your bandwidth and what refills your reserves:

👁️ Optic

1. Visual

Fluorescent tube flicker, blue screen glare, and visual desk clutter.

✦ Antidote: 2200K amber lights, dark mode & sunglasses.
👂 Acoustic

2. Auditory

Leaf blowers, chewing sounds, sudden telephone rings, echoing malls.

✦ Antidote: Loop earplugs, synthetic brown noise, soft lofi.
👕 Somatosensory

3. Tactile

Itchy garment tags, synthetic waistbands, wet shirt cuffs, light tickles.

✦ Antidote: 100% modal cotton, fleece hoodie, weighted lap pad.
🌊 Deep Muscle

4. Proprioception

Motionless sitting, unsupportive ergonomic chairs, physical restlessness.

✦ Antidote: 7–10kg weighted blanket, bear hugs, wall push-ups.
⚖️ Inner Ear

5. Vestibular

Elevator drops, winding taxi rides, rapid visual movement, vertigo.

✦ Antidote: Gentle rocking chairs, floor grounding, hammocks.
🌸 Nasal

6. Olfactory

Heavy elevator perfumes, chemical cleaning agents, pungent heated food.

✦ Antidote: Natural vanilla, fresh pine, pure unscented air.
🍵 Oral Stims

7. Gustatory

Unexpected food chunks, slimy textures, sharp bitterness.

✦ Antidote: Predictable safe foods, crunchy pretzels, warm mugs.
🫀 Autonomic

8. Interoception

Missing thirst and hunger until trembling; postponing the restroom.

✦ Antidote: Scheduled hydration alarms & gentle body scans.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an official diagnosis to use this?

Never. Your sensory thresholds and nervous system boundaries are valid regardless of medical paperwork. If you feel easily drained by crowds, noise, lights, or chaotic schedules, this workbook was made for you.

What if I don't own a printer?

The files are optimized for PDF annotation apps like GoodNotes, Notability, and Samsung Notes on iPad or Android tablets. You can also print selected single sheets at your local print shop.

Why is stimming encouraged in this planner?

Stimming is natural autonomic regulation. Occupational science shows that rhythmic rocking, tactile fidgets, and deep pressure actively lower cortisol and regulate neurotransmitters. We celebrate stimming as self-care.

How do I access the digital files after purchase?

You receive instant, perpetual download access to both US Letter and International A4 vector PDFs, along with instructions and the interactive Web Audio companion link.
